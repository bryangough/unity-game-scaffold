# __GAME__ privacy policy

*Last updated: __DATE__*

__GAME__ does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases.** Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Levels solved, stars, best scores and your settings are saved only on your
  phone or in your browser, and are deleted when you uninstall the game (or clear the site's data in your browser).
- **The phone apps never go online.** The Android app does not ask for internet access; the only permission it asks
  for is vibration, used for the short buzzes as you play (you can turn them off in the settings).
- **The web version** downloads the game and its music from the page that hosts it, like any web page. The game sends
  nothing back.
- **Links:** the credits screen may link to our website. It opens in your browser, under that site's own policy.
